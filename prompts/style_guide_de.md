# German (de-DE) Style Guide (sample)

## Address and tone
- Address users formally with "Sie" and the matching possessives ("Ihr", "Ihre").
- Keep a friendly, professional tone. Avoid slang.

## Numbers, currency, and dates
- Use a decimal comma and a period as the thousands separator: 1.250,00
- Put the currency symbol after the amount, separated by a space: 4,99 €, 9,99 $
- Keep the original currency unless the source asks for a conversion.
- Write dates as DD.MM.YYYY. US source dates are MM/DD/YYYY, so 05/10/2026 is 10.05.2026.
- Write temperatures with a space before the unit: 25 °C

## Punctuation
- Use German quotation marks: „…“

## Protected names (never translate, shorten, or alter)
- NovaPay (brand name, including compounds such as "NovaPay-Konto")
- NovaPay Plus (subscription tier)
- QuickSend (feature name)

## Preferred terminology
| English | German |
|---|---|
| cart | Warenkorb |
| checkout | Kasse |
| sign in | anmelden |
| account | Konto |
| settings | Einstellungen |
| support team | Support-Team |

## Safety and legal content
- Translate warnings, allergen information, dosages, storage limits, and legal or privacy statements completely and literally. Never shorten, soften, or omit them.
